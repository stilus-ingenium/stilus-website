Terms · 04

We state them plainly because they protect your time and ours. If you accept them, continue; if not, we respect your decision.

  1. An invitation, not an offerThis page is for expressing interest only. It is not an offer of securities or an invitation to subscribe.
  2. No details before confidentialityWe share no product information or figure until a non-disclosure agreement, and any other needed agreement, is signed.
  3. No money through the websiteWe receive no money through this page and request none by any means before the legal agreements are signed.
  4. No promised returnInvesting in young companies can lose money as well as make it. We promise no return and no rate.
  5. Proof of funds is requiredFor every tier, without exception. No request is reviewed without proof.
  6. Legal costs are borne by the roundStructuring and closing costs are paid from the round amount at closing. For non-GCC nationals: a SAR 1 million minimum, with foreign-investor registration fees charged to their share.
  7. The decision is the company'sWe accept or decline without giving reasons, and initial acceptance binds neither party.
  8. Negotiation comes after acceptanceWhat is on this page is fixed. Negotiation begins on the agreement's terms after acceptance, not before.
  9. No intermediariesWe deal with the investor or its authorised representative directly. We accept no broker, and neither pay nor take brokerage fees.
  10. Every step through the platformNo calls and no introductory meetings. You move forward step by step, and each step opens once the previous one is complete.

What happens next

What happens next · 06

  1. Confirmation

    An instant reference number, and a link to your email to confirm within 48 hours. Unconfirmed requests are deleted automatically.

  2. Review

    Within 15 days the request is reviewed and the proof of funds checked; the decision reaches your email.

  3. Confidentiality

    On initial acceptance, you receive a link to sign the non-disclosure agreement electronically, on this platform.

  4. Data room

    Your track's file opens as soon as you sign. When you are ready to proceed, you declare it from inside.

Privacy notice

What we collect: identity and contact details, amount and track, and the proof-of-funds document. Why: only to assess eligibility and to contact you about your request. Where: stored encrypted with a cloud infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) whose servers may be outside the Kingdom, accessible only to the company's owners. How long: request details are kept for three years to notify you of future rounds; the proof-of-funds document is deleted after 180 days because it loses its value; a request whose email is not confirmed within 48 hours is deleted entirely. You may ask for deletion at any time. Your rights: access, correction and deletion, by writing to dev@stilus-ingenium.com with your reference number. We never sell or share your data with anyone.
